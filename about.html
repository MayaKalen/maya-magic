My Background

A Bit More About Me

I hold a bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering, and I've spent a bit over seven years since then building, designing, and growing websites for businesses around the world.

Along the way I've worked as a freelance developer and individual contractor, as part of small teams, and alongside agencies, across technologies like WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, HubSpot, Squarespace, Wix, and Go High Level. I'm proficient in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and PHP, so I can custom code inside almost any CMS, and I'm just as comfortable in React on the front end and Node on the back end.

Beyond development, I work in SEO, graphic design, marketing, and automation, and I'm currently learning new AI tools for both design and automation. That's what I mean by Maya Magic: not a trick, just years of craft applied with care, so your site looks beautiful, runs smoothly, and actually moves your business forward. I believe actions speak louder than words, so I'd rather show you my work than just tell you about it.
