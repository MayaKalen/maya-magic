My Background

A Bit More About Me

I hold a bachelor's degree in Information Technology Engineering, and I've spent a bit over seven years since then building, designing, and growing websites for businesses around the world.

Along the way I've worked across freelance projects and in-house teams alike — as a Web/SEO Specialist, a Lead Full-Stack Developer at Prism Digital Marketing, a WordPress Developer at Nexus Growth, and today as a remote web developer working directly with clients wherever they are. Every stop added something: the technical discipline of clean, maintainable code; the strategic thinking that comes from SEO and growth work; and the project-management instincts you only really pick up by shipping real work for real businesses on real deadlines.

That's added up to 300+ projects across WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, HubSpot, Squarespace, and Go High Level, for clients spanning nearly every industry and time zone. What ties it all together, though, is pretty simple — I like building things that work. Not just sites that look good, but systems — stores, funnels, automations, CMS builds — that actually move a business forward.
