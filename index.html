What I Do

My Expertise

🌐

WordPress

Over 7 years designing and developing websites using WordPress. Skilled with Elementor, Divi, and Avada, and plugins like WooCommerce, Yoast SEO, and Contact Forms. I customize themes and plugins with HTML/CSS, optimize sites for performance, and convert PSD designs into fully built WordPress sites.

🛍️

Shopify

I build and customize Shopify themes using Liquid, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, converting Figma designs into stores that look great and run smoothly. I improve site speed, clean up code, fix checkout and product page issues, and set up abandoned cart recovery and product filters to help boost sales.

🎨

Graphic Designing

Logos, flyers, banners, brochures, and templates for ads and emails. I turn Figma or PSD designs into WordPress, Shopify, or custom websites using Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, and Figma — plus social media management and content creation to help businesses grow online.

🧡

HubSpot

Building and managing HubSpot websites, landing pages, and automation, with custom HubL coding for advanced CMS functionality. Seamless integration with HubSpot CRM for automation and lead nurturing, plus A/B testing and analytics for continuous improvement.

⬛

Squarespace

Extensive experience building custom Squarespace sites with a range of features and functions — especially for artists. I've designed sites with memberships, courses, offer pages, and checkout pages, and I'm comfortable adding custom HTML, CSS, and JS.

🔷

Webflow

Strong experience with Webflow CMS, Designer, and Interactions — building responsive, high-performance websites that match design needs. I focus on clean UI/UX, cross-device reliability, and use CSS and JavaScript to ensure great performance and client satisfaction.

🚀

Go High Level

Designing landing pages, funnels, and websites in Go High Level, plus setting up workflows, automations, and email campaigns. I build systems for subscriptions, checkouts, and communities to help boost traffic and grow your business.
