The Process

How the Magic Happens

Every project follows the same spell, cast in four steps, from a first conversation to a site that keeps working long after launch.

✦ ✦ ☁️ 🍃 ✨

Every project passes through four little worlds. Follow the trail from a first hello to a site that keeps on working.

  1. 🌲

    Step 01

    Whispering Woods

    Discovery & Vision

    Understanding your brand, your goals, and the feeling you want your site to hold.

    • A friendly kickoff call
    • Goals, audience and must haves
    • A clear plan and sitemap
  2. 🏜️

    Step 02

    Golden Dunes

    Design & Prototype

    Crafting the look and feel in Figma, blending beauty with usability.

    • Moodboard and style direction
    • Page designs in Figma
    • Your feedback, then polish
  3. ⛰️

    Step 03

    Terracotta Peaks

    Development & Build

    Bringing the design to life on WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, or fully custom code.

    • Responsive on every screen
    • SEO and speed baked in
    • Forms, tools and automations connected
  4. 🌙

    Step 04

    Cocoa Sky

    Launch & Support

    Testing, optimizing, and staying by your side as your business grows.

    • Testing across devices
    • A smooth launch day
    • Ongoing care and updates
